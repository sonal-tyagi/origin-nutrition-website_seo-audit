# origin-nutrition-website_seo-audit

[Origin Nutrition](https://originnutrition.in) is an Indian brand selling plant-based protein powders that are vegan, dairy-free, soy-free and free of added sugar, with 9 flavours to choose from.
Besides its core protein range, it offers nutrition products for seniors and kids, plus accessories such as shaker bottles.

## What I Did

I audited the website and prepared an SEO strategy, shared as two PDFs:

- **Technical audit and keyword strategy:** [Origin Nutrition Audit _ Sonal Tyagi.pdf](https://github.com/sonal-tyagi/origin-nutrition-website_seo-audit/blob/main/Origin%20Nutrition%20Audit%20_%20Sonal%20Tyagi.pdf)
- **On-page plan, Search Console analysis and off-page strategy:** [Origin Nutrition On Page Plan.pdf](https://github.com/sonal-tyagi/origin-nutrition-website_seo-audit/blob/main/Origin%20Nutrition%20On%20Page%20Plan.pdf)

> A case-study submission built from a limited brief, not a full-scale audit.

## Technical Audit

| Issue | Severity | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Breadcrumbs missing | High | Weak site hierarchy; no BreadcrumbList rich results |
| Structured data missing | High | Fewer rich result opportunities |
| Core Web Vitals | High | Hurts page experience and rankings |
| Image alt tags | Low | Lower image search visibility |

**Priority:** fix breadcrumbs and Core Web Vitals first.

## On-Page Plan

Page: `/collections/daily-plant-proteins`

- **URL:** change to `/collections/plant-protein-powder` (higher volume, commercial intent)
- **Meta title:** Plant Based Protein Powder – Vegan & Sugar-Free Range
- **Meta description:** add one (152 characters, mentioning 25g protein and 9 flavours); none exists today
- **H1:** change from "Collection: Daily Plant Protein" to "Plant Based Protein Powder"
- **H2s:** replace interface text (cart, filters, footer) with eight keyword-focused H2s
- **Content:** expand the thin page to about 1,500 words (top competitor runs about 1,400)
- **Internal links:** link to the seniors, kids and shaker collections
- **Schema:** BreadcrumbList, ItemList, Product, FAQPage
- **Conversion:** offer multiple payment options, run Google Ads and add bundle offers

## Search Console Analysis

| Scenario | Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Biggest quick win | "Whey protein price": position 6.1, CTR 3.00% | Optimise title, meta and content; push into the top 3 |
| Traffic drop on `/blog/protein-guide` | Clicks down 67% (1,240 to 410) | Check rankings, indexing, content and competitor changes, internal links |
| Structural problem | Cannibalisation between two whey vs plant protein posts | Consolidate into one page and redirect the weaker URL |
| "Prove it" report | Client says traffic is flat | Report clicks, impressions, average position and CTR |
